Let me tell you how I got here

Hey, I'm Jonathan Verstraeten.

I opened my first business at sixteen. While people my age had weekends, I had staff to manage, inventory to count and rent to cover every month. It taught me early that results come from showing up consistently, not from waiting for the right moment.

I started studying trading in every spare hour I had. For years I lost money, bought courses that delivered nothing, and followed people who turned out not to be trading their own calls. I kept going because I could see the skill was real, even if most of the people selling it weren't.

Eventually it clicked. My trading income passed what the business was making, then passed it again. I sold the business, moved to Dubai and have been trading full-time since. This group exists because I wish something like it had existed when I was starting out.

Frequently asked questions

What if I know nothing about trading?

Most people in the group have never placed a trade. Every position comes with the entry, the target and the stop, and you can follow along without risking anything until you're ready.

Why is it free?

A broker partnership pays for it. If you open an account through our link, the broker pays us a referral fee from their side. You are never charged, and you're under no obligation to open anything.

How do I start and what do I need?

One tap into Telegram. No form, no email, no card. Watch a few sessions and decide from there whether you want to trade for yourself.

Do you trade your own money?

Yes. Every position posted in the group is one I've taken with my own capital. I don't post calls I haven't taken myself.

Will you manage my money for me?

No. We never take deposits and never have access to your account. Your money stays with your broker, under your control, at all times.

Is there any kind of commitment?

None. There's no subscription, no paid tier and no contract. You can leave the group in one tap and nobody will call you.
